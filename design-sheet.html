Navigation

On the hero

Full glass bar, 68px: brand left, sections centre, language switch and yellow CTA right.

Scrolling down

The bar's left edge sweeps right into the CTA while brand, tabs and language slide 28px right and fade. Only the CTA remains, anchored right (never centred), at 42px with a soft drop shadow.

Scrolling up / hover / focus

The full bar returns, slimmer (inset 6px top and bottom). It needs 24px of deliberate scroll in one direction to switch, so it never flickers.

One gesture

Bar and content move together: 860ms, cubic-bezier(.45,0,.15,1). Content never appears before the bar reaches it.

Background · ambient light + grain

Fixed layer under everything: mint ground + five very soft radial glows — aqua #71DAD5 @55%, brand blue #1490C2 @20%, settled warm #FFD678 @22%, periwinkle #788AE2 @14%, teal #2FB6CB @22%. Each drifts on a 58–80s ease-in-out loop and the whole field drifts up half a screen over the full page scroll. On top, a fine film grain (1:1 128px tile, single-pixel specks, 8.5%) that hops a few pixels ~8×/s for a quiet buzz; it stops under reduced motion. Cards and dark sections sit above it untouched. Drift is off under reduced motion.

Motion

Curves

  • Enter: decelerate cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)
  • On-screen change / exit: ease-in-out cubic-bezier(.65,0,.35,1)
  • Nav: cubic-bezier(.45,0,.15,1)
  • Scroll scenes: sine in-out

Timing

  • Press / lift 220ms · exit 360ms
  • Enter 700ms · large moves 900ms
  • Hover fill 640ms · nav 860ms
  • Stagger 110ms
  • Scroll smoothing ≈140ms

Rules

  • Calm and continuous: no bounce, squash, wobble, flip or spin
  • Nothing pops in at full opacity
  • Things recede by scaling and dimming, not by jumping
  • Entrances slower than exits
  • Reduced motion: transitions collapse to instant; background stops

Scroll scenes

  • All scroll-linked and reversible.
  • Deck: the front card rises in one smooth arc clear of the stack, passes behind at the top, sinks into the back slot.
  • Trainable: dots and cards connect, then the scene clears completely.
  • Research: chip rises, then the three findings one by one; they hold for more than a full screen of scroll, then soften, shrink and melt back into the returning dots.
  • Meet: dots gather → sphere glides down → becomes the logo → chip rises → the name grows out from behind the logo down into place → descriptor.
  • Method: cards arrive 4→1, minimise 1→4, slide behind the phone; the stage crossfades into the next section.

What Tarashio is not

YesNoMotivation
  • Starts when the cards are ≥60% in view; resets only after they leave.
  • Toggles flip Yes → No one by one: 500ms lead-in, 420ms apart, 650ms each.
  • A 350ms pause, then all three titles are struck through together (1.3s, ease-in-out) while they drain to grey.
  • The closing line arrives 250ms later.
